# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss evidence and design decisions directly; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and publication of private information. Accept good-faith correction and distinguish critique of a proposal from attacks on a person.

Do not share real incident evidence, secrets, personal data, malware or sensitive third-party target details in public collaboration. No unauthorized testing or retaliation is acceptable in the project's name.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, restrict participation or close contributions that violate these expectations. For private conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on [HungBil's GitHub profile](https://github.com/HungBil). Security vulnerabilities should use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If no suitable private contact is available, avoid publishing sensitive details while requesting a safe channel.
