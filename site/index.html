03 / The long view

Faster attacks.
More deliberate defense.

As AI changes the speed and scale of attacks, defense needs decisions that are faster to reach, safer to execute and possible to verify.

Our research spans conventional and AI-enabled incidents. Protection does not wait for proof that an attacker used AI.

Read the project vision ↗
01

Can it prevent an attack?

First lab: Caddy, Coraza and reviewed CRS before a private application origin. Prove rejection before application harm, bypass resistance and legitimate traffic behavior.

02

Can it act during an attack?

Use evidence to propose an approved ingress maintenance action. Measure harm until verified effect. This does not establish host, identity or egress containment.

03

Can it recover after compromise?

Restore a pinned backup into an isolated application. Check business data, clean-build trust and compromised identities; measure actual RPO and RTO.

04

Where is AI always present?

The resident scheduler tracks changed evidence, freshness and durable state. Real Codex trials now run through CLIProxyAPI with bounded reads and explicit failure. Final assessment quality ties the deterministic baseline; operational AI value remains unproven.
